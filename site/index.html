01

A short-run aggregate association

Across 3,958 maturity-bucket-week observations, larger announced offering amounts are associated with a $9.4536 million contemporaneous increase in aggregate primary-dealer positions per $1 billion of supply.

95% confidence interval

$2.5134 million to $16.3937 million per $1 billion

Monday alignment; bucket and week fixed effects; Bartlett Driscoll–Kraay inference, bandwidth 6. Sample: April 8, 2013 through March 9, 2026. The strict-lag confidence interval includes zero.

This is the retained OPEN-07 v5 headline association. The public presentation uses those retained results; it does not represent a fresh estimation run.

02

Interpretation and timing limits

Descriptive local projections

The binary high-supply indicator uses an expanding p75 threshold that includes the current week. Two of five non-overlapping lead tests are significant. These projections fail clean timing validation and provide descriptive support only.

Regime comparisons

QT and non-QT estimates are descriptive point estimates, not evidence of amplification or a structural Federal Reserve mechanism.

Unidentified outcomes

Terminal holders and later distribution are not identified. These aggregate data do not establish customer identity, deposit settlement, dealer-capacity elasticity, or structural policy causality.

Sources and provenance
